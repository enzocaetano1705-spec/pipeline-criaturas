Show the creature calmly poised JUST BEFORE a burst, without action effects obscuring the anatomy.

Anatomy/design: recognizably chelonian, low and compact but lean, ONE broad oval dorsal shell tapered toward the rear, ONE natural ivory ventral plastron, short thick retractile neck, rounded turtle head with a subtle short beak and attentive half-lidded amber eyes. Keep the four limbs anatomically distinct and legible with natural occlusion, not extra arms or floating hands. The body is mostly horizontal, not an upright human torso.
